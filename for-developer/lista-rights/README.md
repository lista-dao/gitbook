# Lista Rights

Reward distribution for the **holder** emission programme — LISTA emissions routed to eligible holders rather than to lending or credit positions.

`LendingRewardsDistributorV2` and `RewardsRouter` are each deployed more than once, one per emission programme, and the address tables do not always say which. Identify the contract by the page it sits on: this section for holder emission, [BSC Core](../lista-lending/smart-contract-bsc-core.md) for lending and bStock, [BSC Credit](../lista-lending/smart-contract-bsc-credit.md) for credit.

* [Smart Contract](smart-contract.md) — the holder-emission deployment.
