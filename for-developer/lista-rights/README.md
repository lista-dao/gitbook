# Lista Rights

Reward distribution for the **holder** emission programme — the LISTA Holder Boost, extra emissions layered on top of eligible lending deposits/borrows and weighted by the holder's LISTA-balance tier (see [Holder Benefits](../../governance/lista/lista-holder-benefits.md)), not a reward paid instead of a lending/credit position.

`LendingRewardsDistributorV2` and `RewardsRouter` are each deployed several times, and the address tables do not always say for which emission programme. Identify the contract by the page it sits on: this section for holder emission, [BSC Core](../lista-lending/smart-contract-bsc-core.md) for lending and bStock, [BSC Credit](../lista-lending/smart-contract-bsc-credit.md) for credit.

* [Smart Contract](smart-contract.md) — the holder-emission deployment.
