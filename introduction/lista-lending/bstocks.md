# bStocks

Lista Lending supports tokenized equities (bStocks) as collateral, allowing users to borrow stablecoins against on-chain stock exposure while retaining their underlying positions.

bStocks available as collateral are on Lista Lending: CRCLB, MUB, NVDAB, SNDKB, TSLAB, SPCXB, ALABB, ASMLB, ASTSB, BMNRB, COHRB, CRDOB, IRENB, NFLXB, SMCIB, USARB, QQQB, SKHYB, SPYB, METAB, ARMB, HOODB, WDCB, IBMB, AAOIB, RKLBB, NOKB, GLWB, SOXLB, BABAB, QCOMB, CBRSB, GOOGLB, NBISB, COINB, DRAMB, MSTRB, MSFTB, MRVLB, AMATB, TSMB, TQQQB, AVGOB, LITEB, AAPLB, FLNCB, KORUB, AMDB, MUUB, AINTWB, MVLLB, PLTRB, AXTIB, GMEB, CRWVB, ORCLB, SMHB, GSB, QNTB, DELLB, PYPLB, AMZNB, SOXSB, BEB, SNXXB, EWYB, INTCB.&#x20;

Available loans against these assets are: USDT, USD1, U, and USDC (for certain markets).

bStocks Lending works the same as Lista Lending, available 7/24. bStocks Lending gets its price feeds from Atlas Oracle.

Please note that Lista reserves the right to pause certain functions for certain markets in case of emergency.
