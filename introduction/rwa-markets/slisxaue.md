# slisXAUE

slisXAUE is a yield-bearing gold token issued by Lista DAO in partnership with XAUE Protocol, it is obtained by depositing XAUT on Lista DAO. slisXAUE carries a native yield of roughly 3% APR.&#x20;

### slisXAUE Gold Campaign Phase 1

For 60 days starting from August 10th 2026, users can deposit to be one of the top 10 addresses by time-weighted average holding to each independently earn an additional 5% APY (up to 8% APY) on top of slisXAUE's native yield. Per-address rewards are capped at $1,000 XAUT.&#x20;

The baseline for participation is 20 slisXAUE, held on average across the full 60-day campaign. THe average is calculated using a time-weighted average: every address gets a daily snapshot (at a random, undisclosed time), and your qualifying holding is the average across all 60 days.

Reward = (your average holding, converted to USD at settlement-day price) × 5% × (60 / 365) \*capped at $1,000 XAUT per address.&#x20;

Total exposure across all 10 spots is hard-capped at $10,000 XAUT. If there’s a tie for the 10th spot, users that are tied will get an equal split of whatever budget remains after the non-tied addresses are paid.&#x20;
